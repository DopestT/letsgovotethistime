WHAT WE STORE — AND WHAT WE DON'T

ANONYMOUS
CHECK-IN PRIVACY.

This map is not official turnout. It is a visualization of voluntary, anonymous, self-reported check-ins.

What one check-in stores

  • A one-way hash of a random browser/device token so the same browser updates one check-in instead of creating another.
  • A one-way hash of the network IP used only for duplicate/bot-rate controls. The raw IP address is not stored in the check-in table.
  • Whether the anonymous check-in says Not Yet or Voted.
  • The selected state and, if voluntarily entered, only the first three digits of the ZIP code. The full ZIP code is discarded by the server.
  • Basic partner/UTM attribution when the visitor arrived through a distribution link.
  • Timestamps needed to operate and protect the system.

What we do not ask for or store

  • Name, account, phone number, or email address for the check-in.
  • Exact GPS coordinates, street address, polling-place address, or precise device location.
  • Party, candidate, ideology, intended vote, ballot selections, or how someone voted.
  • Voter-file matching or cross-site advertising identifiers.

Duplicate and abuse controls

One random browser/device token represents one check-in. A Not Yet status can later become Voted; once marked Voted, the status is final for that anonymous browser identity. To reduce scripted duplication, new-device creation from the same network is rate-limited. Shared households and networks are therefore not treated as a single voter, but abnormal bursts are slowed.

Map privacy

The public map will use aggregate or deliberately coarse geography. It will not place a dot at an individual's real home, device, or polling-place coordinates. Small geographic buckets can be withheld until enough check-ins exist to avoid displaying a highly specific one-person area.

What the numbers mean

“Voted” percentages are percentages of people who voluntarily checked in through this site. They are not official turnout rates, scientific polling, voter-registration records, or proof that an individual ballot was cast.
