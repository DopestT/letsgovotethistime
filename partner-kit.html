03
DISTRIBUTION STANDARDS

KEEP IT
LEGITIMATE.

The growth goal is broad access, not manipulation. Partners should treat the site as a civic utility and preserve its neutral boundary.

  • Use official election sources. The tool routes voters to election officials for current location, hours, identification rules, and voting options.
  • Transportation stays self-service. The voter chooses and books directly with Uber, Lyft, transit, taxi, paratransit, or another provider. Let's Go Vote This Time does not dispatch, fund, reimburse, or guarantee rides.
  • Do not promise a rideshare promotion. Uber and Lyft election programs and discounts can change by election, market, eligibility, and provider terms. Only share a current offer confirmed by the provider.
  • No political-preference profiling. Do not collect or append party, candidate, ideology, or intended-vote data to partner links.
  • No fabricated endorsements. Do not imply sponsorship by the EAC, Vote.gov, a state election office, Uber, Lyft, or another company unless a real agreement exists.
  • No spam or deceptive acquisition. Share through communities, newsletters, organizations, events, and accounts where the message is relevant and permitted.
  • Protect voter privacy. Do not place polling-place addresses, voter records, or personal political information in tracking parameters.
TRANSPORTATION BOUNDARY

WE HELP WITH THE PLAN.
THE PROVIDER HANDLES THE RIDE.

Partners should point voters toward official election information and self-service transportation choices. Ride booking, payment, dispatch, driver support, accessibility fulfillment, cancellations, refunds, and service guarantees remain between the voter and the transportation provider.
