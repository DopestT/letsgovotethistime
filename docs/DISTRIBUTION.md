# Legitimate Distribution Standard

## Objective

Grow Let's Go Vote This Time as a nonpartisan voter-access utility without spam, deceptive acquisition, fabricated endorsements, political-preference profiling, or misuse of voter data.

## Primary distribution surfaces

1. Civic and community organizations that can share the voter tool with their existing audiences.
2. Local institutions and community events where voting-location and transportation planning are directly useful.
3. Newsletter and social distribution using the public partner kit.
4. Search discovery for people explicitly looking for voting-location and transportation-planning help.
5. Transportation, accessibility, and ride-access partnerships handled through the existing partner-reply workflow.

## Partner link standard

Use:

`https://letsgovotethistime.com/?partner=<partner>&utm_source=partner&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=2026-voting-plan`

Rules:

- `partner` identifies the organization or distribution source only.
- Never include party, candidate, ideology, intended vote, race, religion, health information, or other sensitive personal attributes in URL parameters.
- Do not put voter-file identifiers, email addresses, phone numbers, polling-place addresses, or other personal data in tracking parameters.
- Preserve the canonical site URL for indexing.

## Message standard

Distribution copy should focus on three factual actions:

- verify the official voting location;
- make a transportation plan;
- help someone else make a plan.

Do not imply that Let's Go Vote This Time is itself an election authority. Do not claim a partnership or endorsement unless an agreement actually exists.

## Outreach workflow

Partnership outreach may use the existing reply-capable partner workflow documented in `REPLY_ENGINE.md`. Inbound replies should be summarized conservatively. A polite reply is not a commitment.

High-impact or consequential agreements — including paid media, transportation-company commitments, public endorsements, or material spending — remain approval-gated.

## SEO standard

Create only pages that answer a real voter or partner need. Avoid thin state/city pages, duplicated templates, keyword stuffing, fake activity, or mass-generated pages with no unique value.

Minimum technical foundation:

- canonical URLs;
- descriptive title and meta description;
- Open Graph/Twitter metadata;
- `robots.txt`;
- XML sitemap;
- crawlable internal links;
- official-source links for election information.

## Measurement

Partner and campaign parameters should remain stable so analytics can be added without breaking previously distributed links. Measure useful outcomes such as official-lookup opens, route-plan starts, shares, ride-access inquiries, and partnership inquiries rather than vanity traffic alone.
