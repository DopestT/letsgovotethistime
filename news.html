WHAT THIS FEED IS

The feed indexes reporting from a curated set of major publishers using GDELT's documented news-search API. Headlines, publication times, source domains, and article links are preserved so readers can go directly to the underlying reporting.

WHAT IT IS NOT

This page does not treat one publisher as the official truth, does not infer facts from headline volume, and does not replace original reporting. A source disappearing from the feed means the index could not retrieve it in the current window—not that the event did not happen.

Coverage availability depends on publisher indexing and GDELT ingestion. Some publishers use subscriptions or paywalls. Let's Go Vote This Time does not bypass publisher access controls.
