You keep hearing the same thing: Go vote.

But that skips the part that decides whether voting actually happens.

  • Do you know where you are voting?
  • Do you know when you are going?
  • Do you know how you are getting there?
  • And when you do vote, can your community see that people are actually showing up?

That is what Let's Go Vote This Time is built to solve.

MAKE THE PLAN.

Use official election information to verify what you need. Decide when you intend to vote. If you are voting in person, work out transportation before the last minute.

And if you want a little accountability, sign up for no more than four useful election reminders. Then we stop.
